ABOUT THE GAME

Arcade Battle

Four games. Three ways to play. One shared room for your next challenge.

01

Bingo Battle

Arrange a private 5×5 or 10×10 board with unique numbers. Each called number marks both players’ boards; complete five lines to win. If both reach five lines on the same call, the round is a draw.

02

SOS

Choose a 5×5 or 10×10 grid. Place S or O to build S-O-S horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Each new word scores a point and earns another turn.

03

Dots & Boxes

Connect neighboring dots and claim completed boxes. Closing a box scores a point and lets you play again. Choose a 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, or 10×10 box grid.

04

Disappearing Tic-Tac-Toe

Keep at most three pieces per player. When you place a fourth, your oldest disappears. Make three in a row before your marks vanish.

Ways to play

Play together on one device, challenge an Easy/Medium/Hard bot, or connect two devices in an online room. Online players can switch games in the same room and chat directly with each other. Select a 15-second, 30-second, or 1-minute turn timer.

Online rooms and chat require an internet connection. Score history is stored in each browser, not in a central account.
